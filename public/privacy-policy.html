Privacy Policy

The Connect Pharma is a B2B pharmaceutical business platform. We use account and business information to provide sign-in, role-based portals, business document review, marketplace enquiries, subscriptions, payments, security and support.

Information handled

Depending on how you use the service, information may include your name, business email, phone number, account role and status, business licence and GST documents, catalogue and enquiry details, payment references, administrator review notes, and security or audit events. Payment card credentials are handled by the payment provider; the application receives payment status and related references.

The Contact Us form prepares an email draft in your browser. The website does not transmit or store that draft; if you choose to send it using your email app, the message is handled through your email provider and delivered to our support address.

Use and protection

Information is used to operate and protect the requested business services, review submitted documents, process payments, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Business documents are intended for private administrative review and are not published as public catalogue files. Access and retention depend on account status, service configuration and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Your responsibilities and requests

Submit only information and documents you are authorised to provide. Do not upload personal identity documents through the business KYC form. For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact us. Some records may need to be retained where required for payment, security or legal purposes.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact connectpharma@gmail.com. This notice should be reviewed against the services and applicable Indian data-protection requirements before production use.
